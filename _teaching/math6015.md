### Course description

This course introduces the mathematical foundations, models, and algorithms of convex optimization. Topics include convex sets and functions, convex optimization problems, approximation and fitting, unconstrained and equality-constrained minimization, interior-point methods, statistical estimation, geometric problems, and selected first-order optimization methods.

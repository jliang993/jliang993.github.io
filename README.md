# Jingwei Liang (梁经纬)

I am a tenure-track Associate Professor at the [Institute of Natural Sciences](https://ins.sjtu.edu.cn/) and the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.sjtu.edu.cn/), [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/). I am also a member of the [Lab for Imaging, Machine Learning and Optimization (LIMO)](https://limo-sjtu.github.io/).

[Personal website](https://jliang993.github.io/) · [Publications](https://jliang993.github.io/publications/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aaIkvG0AAAAJ&hl=en) · [GitHub](https://github.com/jliang993)

## Research

My research lies at the intersection of mathematical optimization, data science, and image processing. My main interests include:

- nonsmooth optimization;
- mathematical data science;
- mathematical image processing and inverse problems.

My work develops mathematical foundations and efficient algorithms for structured optimization and data-driven imaging problems.

## Academic background

- **2021–present:** Tenure-track Associate Professor, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China
- **2020–2021:** Lecturer in Mathematical Data Science, Queen Mary University of London, UK
- **2017–2020:** Postdoctoral Research Associate, University of Cambridge, UK
- **2013–2017:** PhD in Mathematics, Université de Caen Normandie, France
